# Governance

WinDevKit is a single-maintainer project. The maintainer (see
[`MAINTAINERS.md`](MAINTAINERS.md)):

- reviews and merges all changes,
- decides what ships and cuts every release,
- and settles disagreements about direction.

Decisions that change how the project works are recorded where
they take effect — new resources and their reasons in
[`PAIN_POINTS.md`](PAIN_POINTS.md), shipped changes in
[`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md), and direction in
[`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md). The project is MIT licensed, free,
and developed in the open; forks are welcome under the license
terms.
